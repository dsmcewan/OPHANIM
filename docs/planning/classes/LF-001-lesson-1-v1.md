## Learning objective and reason

OPHANIM will distinguish the desired outcome from a proposed method, state observable success and the learning-only boundary, and identify consequential ambiguity before selecting tasks, tools, rooms, or people.

This is the prerequisite for later judgments about evidence, necessary work, dependencies, feasibility, and verification. Structural correctness cannot compensate for solving the wrong problem.

## Unassisted learner prompt

> This is a hypothetical learning exercise. Six people need a one-hour discussion of a draft. All six can attend from 14:00 to 15:00, and any proposed room must accommodate six people. You are examining what a plan would require; you are not arranging the meeting.
>
> Before choosing a room, tool, person or task, state the observable outcome that would count as success and identify any part of that outcome the supplied facts do not yet establish.

The coach presents this prompt unchanged. The coordinator preserves the first response before any diagnostic question, hint, correction, or worked example.

## Diagnostic purpose

Determine whether OPHANIM:

- separates an outcome from candidate means;
- can make success observable without crossing into execution; and
- treats consequential missing information as unknown.

Only after the unassisted attempt may the coach select one diagnostic question based on an observed gap. This package supplies no advance diagnostic wording or answer.

## Published rubric

| Criterion | Supported when | Contradicted when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `C1 objective_method` | The response states the desired result independently of a room, tool, person, or task sequence. | A candidate method or implementation activity is presented as the objective. |
| `C2 observable_success_boundary` | The response gives a condition whose occurrence or absence could be checked and explicitly preserves the hypothetical, learning-only boundary. | Success is purely aspirational, uncheckable, or requires arranging the meeting. |
| `C3 consequential_unknown` | The response identifies at least one unstated fact and explains how it changes whether success is established. | Missing information is assumed favorable, or an immaterial detail is listed without explaining its consequence. |

Equivalent reasoning is accepted; no preferred wording is required. Document creation, task count, or confidence does not satisfy a criterion.

## Evidence vocabulary

- `supplied_fact`: stated directly in the immutable prompt.
